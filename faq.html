Ordering and delivery

Where do you deliver?

Across Guwahati, within about 20 km of the city centre (PIN codes starting with 781). At checkout you pin your address on the map so our rider finds you.

Outside Guwahati, order our 1 kg pack on Blinkit, BigBasket or Flipkart Minutes where available. For bulk orders and events anywhere, ask us for a quote.

When will my order arrive?

You choose a 1-hour delivery slot between 10 am and 10 pm, any day up to 7 days ahead. The earliest slot is at least 1 hour after you order, so your ice arrives frozen.

How much is delivery?

Free on orders of ₹299 or more, for example 4 × 1 kg packs for ₹320.

Below ₹299, delivery is ₹99. Your cart shows how much more to add for free delivery, so 4 packs (₹320, delivered free) cost less than 3 packs with delivery (₹339).

Can I order on WhatsApp instead?

Yes. Fill in your cart and tap Order On WhatsApp Instead. WhatsApp opens with your order typed out. Send it, and pay when your ice arrives.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes, free of charge, up to 2 hours before your delivery slot starts. WhatsApp or call us on +91 60033 68214 with your order number. After that we can move your order to another slot. See Refunds & Cancellations.

Our ice

What makes your ice different?

It is made from RO + UV + UF purified water and frozen to be clear and dense. Clear, dense ice melts more slowly, so your drink stays cold without getting watered down, and it has no taste or smell of its own.

Is it safe to eat and drink?

Yes. Our ice is food-grade and made under FSSAI licence 20323101002084.

How should I store it?

Put it in the freezer as soon as it arrives and keep the bag sealed, so it doesn't pick up smells from other food. For parties, keep it in an insulated ice box and take out only what you need.

What if my ice arrives melted?

It shouldn't: it leaves our freezer just before your slot and travels in insulated packing. A little water on the outside of the bag, or cubes lightly stuck together, is normal. Tap the bag to separate them and freeze it.

Water inside the bag or cubes fused into a block means it melted, and it's on us. Hand it back to the rider for a fresh pack in the next slot, or send a photo on WhatsApp within 2 hours for a free replacement or a full refund. Details.

Do you make artisanal ice?

Yes: cubes, spheres, pillars, diamonds, shot glasses, roses, ice with your logo, ice with flowers or fruit frozen inside, and punch blocks. Browse the catalog or add them from the store. Custom pieces are made to order.

Payment and invoices

How can I pay?

Online with UPI, cards, netbanking or wallets through Razorpay, our secure payment partner. If you order on WhatsApp, you pay on delivery.

Will I get a GST invoice?

Yes. Prices include 5% GST, and your GST invoice is emailed to you after payment. You can also download it from your order page. Our GSTIN is 18ADRPG9635P1ZJ.

Do you supply bars, hotels and events?

Yes, in any quantity. Tell us what you need with the bulk enquiry form or on WhatsApp, and we will send you a quote.
