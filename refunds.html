Refunds & Cancellations

Last updated 24 November 2026

Ice is highly perishable, so check it the moment it reaches you. If it isn't right, it's on us: a fresh pack or your money back, with no forms.

What if my ice arrives melted?

It shouldn't. Your ice leaves our freezer just before your slot and travels in insulated packing, so it reaches you frozen.

Normal: a little water on the outside of the bag, or cubes lightly stuck together. That's frost thawing on the surface. Tap the bag on a counter to separate the cubes, then put it in the freezer.

Melted: water inside the bag, or cubes fused into one block. That's our problem to fix:

  • At the door: hand the pack back to our rider. We bring a fresh one in the next available slot, free.
  • Right after delivery: if you spot it once the rider has left, send a photo on WhatsApp (+91 60033 68214) straight away, with your order number. Choose a free replacement or a full refund for that pack.

Ice starts to melt the moment it's out of a freezer, so we can only accept melted-ice claims made when you receive your order, not later. Please put your ice in the freezer as soon as it arrives.

Problems with your order

  • Melted, torn or leaking packs: a free replacement or a full refund, as above.
  • Wrong or missing items: we deliver what is missing, or refund it.
  • We miss your slot: we will call you. You can pick a new slot, or cancel for a full refund including the delivery charge.

Cancelling an order

  • Cancel free of charge up to 2 hours before your delivery slot starts. WhatsApp or call us with your order number.
  • Within 2 hours of your slot, or once your order is out for delivery, we can move it to another slot but can't cancel it.
  • Custom orders (branded ice, infused ice, punch blocks and large event orders) can't be cancelled once we start making them. The quote we send you will say when that is.
  • If an item is out of stock after you pay, we will tell you and refund it in full.

How refunds are paid

  • Online payments are refunded to the same card, UPI or account you paid with, through our payment partner Razorpay.
  • We start the refund within 2 working days of approving it. Your bank usually shows it within 5–7 working days.
  • Orders paid on delivery are refunded by UPI or bank transfer.

Orders from Blinkit, BigBasket or Flipkart Minutes

These are refunded by the app you ordered from, under its own policy. Contact that app's support.
