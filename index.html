Why clear ice melts slower

Cloudy ice is full of trapped air and tiny cracks. Ours is frozen slowly from purified water, so it comes out clear, solid and dense.

  • No trapped air. Solid ice holds its cold longer than ice full of bubbles, and it doesn't crack when your drink hits it.
  • Less surface, slower melt. One large clear cube or sphere touches less of your drink than a handful of small cubes.
  • No watered-down drinks. Your drink stays cold and tastes the same from the first sip to the last.
