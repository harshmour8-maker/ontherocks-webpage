Why clear ice?

It looks the part. Clear ice makes a drink look more elegant and professional, and shows off the colour of what's in the glass.

It's pure. Made from RO + UV + UF purified water, with fewer air bubbles and impurities than cloudy ice.

It melts slowly and evenly. Your drink stays cold without getting watered down, so it keeps the flavour and balance it was made with.
