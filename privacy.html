Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

We only collect what we need to deliver your ice and send your invoice. We never sell your information.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and email address.
  • Your delivery address, PIN code and the map location you pin.
  • What you order, your delivery slot and your invoice details.
  • Messages you send us through the bulk enquiry form, WhatsApp or email.

Payments are handled by Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.

How we use it

  • To deliver your order and contact you about it.
  • To email your receipt and GST invoice, and keep the records tax law requires.
  • To reply to your enquiries.

Who we share it with

Only the services that help us run the shop, and only what each needs:

  • Razorpay, for payments.
  • Resend, which sends our emails.
  • Google Maps or OpenStreetMap, to find your address on the map.
  • Vercel and Upstash, which host our website and order records.
  • Our delivery staff, who see your name, phone number and address.

We may also share information when the law requires it.

On your device

Our website saves your cart and delivery details in your browser so you don't lose them if you leave the page. We don't use advertising trackers.

How long we keep it

We keep order and invoice records for as long as Indian tax law requires (currently about 6 years), then delete them.

Your choices

Email info@frostworkscorp.com to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. We will reply within 30 days. Records we must keep for tax are kept until the law allows us to delete them.
