Delivery Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

We deliver across Guwahati in a 1-hour slot you choose, every day from 10 am to 10 pm.

Where we deliver

  • Guwahati, within about 20 km of the city centre (PIN codes starting with 781). You pin your address on the map at checkout so our rider finds you.
  • Outside Guwahati, order our 1 kg pack on Blinkit, BigBasket or Flipkart Minutes where available.
  • Bulk orders and events anywhere: ask us for a quote.

Delivery slots

  • Choose any 1-hour slot between 10 am and 10 pm, up to 7 days ahead.
  • The earliest slot is at least 1 hour after you order, so we can deliver your ice frozen.
  • We deliver within the slot you choose. If we are running late, we will call or WhatsApp you.

Delivery charges

  • Free delivery on orders of ₹500 or more.
  • Below ₹500, delivery is ₹99. The cart shows how much more to add for free delivery.
  • Orders of ₹1,200 or more get a flat 10% off the ice, applied automatically in the cart.
  • Pan-India: we deliver bulk and event orders anywhere in India. Get a quote and tell us where and how much you need.
  • Delivery charges have no GST. Ice prices already include 5% GST.

When your ice arrives

Ice is packed to stay frozen on the way. Please check it on delivery and put it in the freezer straight away. If anything is wrong, see our Refunds & Cancellations policy.

Your GST invoice is emailed to you after payment and can be downloaded from your order page.
